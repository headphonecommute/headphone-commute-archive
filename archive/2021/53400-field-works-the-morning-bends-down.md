*Maples, Ash, and Oaks: Cedars Instrumentals* is a soundtrack for "*a long walk in the woods*", composed on pedal steel, oud, guitar, hurdy-gurdy, and piano. Released on **Temporary Residence Ltd.** as a compliment album by **Field Works**, titled *Cedars*, the fifteen new tracks invite the listener to stroll along inside the forest. Inspired by the [Slow TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slow_television) movement, the producer behind the project, **Stuart Hyatt** invited **Anna Powell Denton** to film the hike in a single, unedited, real-time shot, with a handheld camera. The video was filmed at Green's Bluff Nature Preserve, a 563-acre wooded habitat in Owen County, Indiana, and spans the entire 33-minutes of the album. The music also incorporates field recordings of a Welsh countryside captured by **Harrison Ridley**. I have enjoyed it very much, watching it on a big screen, while the music was gently swaying in the background, occasionally glancing at the new uncovered scenery, as if I'm floating through the forest on my own. Today I am premiering an edited two-and-a-half-minute opening, and if you like to enter further, you can experience the full film [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LqgNyqXoNUk).

> For this special limited-edition release, Stuart Hyatt deconstructed each track from Cedars and rebuilt them one layer at a time. He removed the narrators’ voices and invited Julien Marchal to introduce piano as the new protagonist.

Besides my dear friend, **Julien Marchal**, on the piano, the list of performers on the album is rather extensive, and includes **Youmna Saba, Dena El Saffar, Danny Paul Grody, Bob Hoffnar, Stuart Hyatt, Tomás Lozano, Nathan Bowles, Alex Roldan, Fadi Tabbal,** and **Marisa Anderson**. The whole release is quite an undertaking, which is bestowed with iridescent Mother of Pearl with Green undertone, beautiful coloured vinyl. Grab yours [here](https://fieldworks.bandcamp.com/album/maples-ash-and-oaks-cedars-instrumentals), while it lasts.
