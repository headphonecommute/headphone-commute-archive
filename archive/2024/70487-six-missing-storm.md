In December of last year, [I premiered a track](../2023/64567-six-missing-guidance.md) from a new [to me] composer from Austin, recording under the peculiarly named **Six Missing** project. I later learned that the name is a tribute to the six people who were unaccounted for near the site of the Revolutionary War’s Battle of Brandywine and the almost supernatural experience that one day **TJ Dumser** had in a cabin around the same area (you can read about it more [here](https://www.sixmissing.com/about)). Dumser's music has always been full of emotion, this time capturing feelings triggered by an unexpected mental health crisis when one is nearly paralyzed with anxiety, burn-out and stress. One can only imagine what a panic attack could feel like, but for those who have been there, you know it is powerful and very real. The piece I am sharing with you today, titled "Storm", happens to capture this oncoming phenomenon of our internal weather, full of its inevitable and uncontrollable takeover of body and mind. But beyond its reflection on life's lower points, it is also encouraging in its sound and message, as the ominous multi-timbral and textural pads slowly break into a positive light of hope - a transformation of fighting through this human condition, withstanding its ups and downs, and then coming out being okay.

> I really had no idea how much this record would truly mean to me and my mental health. What began as exercises to attempt to make myself feel a little bit better each day became a cathartic and ultimately therapeutic endeavor. By the end of the sessions for this record I was able to step back and look at the arc; struggling and living with anxiety and depression as a result of burnout. I was nervous to put it out into the world, but realized I had actually captured something special and know how many people struggle with what I struggle with. So my hope is that if these pieces made \*me\* feel better, perhaps they can help \*others\* as well.
>
> *-- TJ Dumser*

"Storm" is the first single from the six-track EP, titled *Gentle Breath*, which you can look forward to in December via **Nettwerk Music Group**. This single is out tomorrow, October 11th, and you can buy or pre-save the stream [here](https://sixmissing.ffm.to/storm). It's a raw and honest journey that captures both the hollow moments of a struggle and the flickers of hope that keep us kicking along...
