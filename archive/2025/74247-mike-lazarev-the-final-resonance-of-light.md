### Release Notes

**Label:** Dronarivm\
**Release:** Tarnished Tapes and Saturated Signals\
**Date:** May 23rd, 2025\
**Mastered By:** James Murray

[Bandcamp](https://dronarivm.bandcamp.com/album/tarnished-tapes-and-saturated-signals)

Believe it or not, I am not that great at self-promotion. So when it comes to using this very platform, I hesitate and stall. When it comes to shouting into the world about my music, I often feel a bit self-conscious. I'm sure that musicians understand what it takes to finally "release" their work into the world. It's both - a ball of tangled tension and a sigh of great relief. And so today, I finally get over that hurdle by announcing my new album on the esteemed **Dronarivm** imprint. The roots of *Tarnished Tapes and Saturated Signals* are deeply intertwined with those of *Sacred Tonalities* (**Past Inside The Present**, 2023). In fact, a couple of pieces are complex reworks and further explorations of themes. All of it is still a little hazy, deconstructed, and concealed. Not because I'm hiding something, but because you need to scratch at the layered surface to understand and know the truth. Most of you will have no trouble, as I hope my sounds will resonate with something that you also pushed inside. In the end, my curated journey through deconstructed electronica, cinematic ambience, and modern classical sound fuses into expansive nostalgia, reductionist pianism, and deep emotional atmosphere, hopefully evoking a sense of loss, disintegration, and the fragility of our temporal selves. And now I can move on...

> Having broken free from the prison of time, I can freely revisit its very beginnings. This is reflected not only in my production process, where I carefully sculpted each sound within a heavily layered texture, lo-fi aesthetic, and analogue recording, but also in the central idea behind each piece. To me, this music brings to mind the hazy memories of playing an old cassette found in the back of my car, its tape warped and distorted by the heat of that last carefree summer, floating on empty roads towards the sunrise, a ringing in the ears still present after a warehouse rave.

The album was beautifully mastered by **James Murray** and is available today, May 23rd, as a compact disc or a digital download via the label's [Bandcamp](https://dronarivm.bandcamp.com/album/tarnished-tapes-and-saturated-signals). Available on all streaming platforms at the end of this month.
