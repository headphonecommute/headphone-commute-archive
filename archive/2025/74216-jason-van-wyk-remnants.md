South African **Jason van Wyk** is quickly becoming a favourite and prominent artist on these pages. You may even think I'm biased, because I asked Jason to rework [one of my tracks](https://mikelazarev.bandcamp.com/album/saturated-reworks). But this relationship is indeed in reverse - I only requested his participation because I admire his sound, and am indeed inspired by his music when I choose to work on my own. There's something ethereal, delicate, and dreamlike in how his cinematic pads swirl in a cocktail spatial ambience and pulsating rhythm that takes over my heart and bewitches my soul. Stunning harmonic progressions show off true musicianship, while the layered textures keep me occupied with intelligent sound design. This is the culmination of elements that I highly respect in my music. This is why I keep listening to Jason's music and try to share it with the world. This is why, I'm sure, Mike Cadoo continues to invite him on his label. We last heard from Jason nearly three years ago [time is always speeding up], when in October of 2022, he released *Descendants*, which inevitably landed on my Best of the Year list in the most coveted category, *Music For The Film Behind Closed Eyelids.* This is a sacred space, often occupied by the likes of **Max Richter**, **Ólafur Arnalds**, and, of course, **Jóhann Jóhannsson**. With his latest sixth full-length album, *Inherent*, Jason is back on **n5MD**, pushing his boundaries forward, featuring even more expansive soundscapes of warm ambiance, haunting piano melodies, and trance-inducing beats.

> “Remnants” started off life as a demo during the production of my 2022 album Descendants. As the album took shape, the track was set aside as it didn’t quite fit with the overall sound I was aiming for. I rediscovered the demo last year and thought it had a lot of potential for a rework. After a few weeks with it, with most of that time spent on the drums, the track was finally completed.
>
> *-- Jason van Wyk*
