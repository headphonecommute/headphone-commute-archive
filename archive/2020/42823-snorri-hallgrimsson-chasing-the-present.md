### Release Notes

**Label:** Moderna\
**Release:** Chasing The Present\
**Release Date:** February 21st, 2020

[chasingthepresent.com](https://www.chasingthepresent.com/)

Today I am running with an exclusive premiere of a piece by **Snorri Hallgrímsson** from an upcoming release by the same title on **Moderna**. "Chasing the Present" is the title track from the soundtrack for the award-winning documentary by **Mark Waters**, in which he follows James Sebastiano, "a materially successful young man," as he battles crippling anxiety. Hallgrímsson's compositions, produced by **Ólafur Arnalds**, create an atmosphere that is at once, introspective, personal, and deeply poignant. I'm looking forward to connecting with the soundtrack as a whole, but for now, I'll leave you with this single teaser.

> Hallgrímsson's music makes use of filtered strings and pianos to emphasize his depression - but in this stunningly beautiful track, the filter opens completely to showcase the mind's enormous potential when it breaks free from perpetual anxiety.

Although occasionally, I get overwhelmed by thoughts of our brief existence, and perhaps that is a form of mild anxiety, I too am in a constant state of inner battle, trying to be here, right now, chasing the present...
