### Release Notes

**Label:** Home Normal\
**Release:** *Adrift (Remastered + Remixed)*\
**Date:** December 10, 2020\
**Mastered By:** Ian Hawgood\
**Artwork By:** Giulio Aldinucci

[homenormal.com](https://www.homenormal.com/)

This morning I am spending some time in the company of **Pleq** (that's Bartosz Dziadosz) and **Hakobune** (Takahiro Yorifuji). Their album, *Adrift*, was originally released on the **Nomadic Kids Republic** sublabel of **Home Normal**, back in 2012. And now, it's not only remastered [and textured] by **Ian Hawgood** but also complemented with reworks by **Olan Mill**, **Polar M**, and the remix I am premiering for you today by Jason Corder, aka **offthesky**. So yeah, a bunch of characters on this one. *Adrift* is an album of organic ambiance, warm texture, and minuscule glitch that sounds as gorgeous and regal as it did eight years ago. Well, maybe a little more regal. For this edition, Ian has painstakingly reworked the pieces "*from the original masters to reel-to-reel for a wholly new, more open, and nuanced experience.*" All that expensive analog gear put to good use there, eh? The result is a showcase of modern ambiance that still sounds fresh and new, with Corder's remix adding in a touch of sub-particle-sized arrangement in subtle sonics and reminiscent din. The whole package is rounded off with brand new cover art by **Giulio Aldinucci**, which is too beautiful not to include here in its larger format. Thank heavens for Home Normal.
