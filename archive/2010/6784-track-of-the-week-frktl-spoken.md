This week we feature a track by one of our old time friends and contributors, [**Sarah Badr**](http://pieces-at-random.com/). Sarah has previously written a review of **Telefon Tel Aviv**'s [*Immolate Yourself*](../2009/1409-telefon-tel-aviv-immolate-yourself.md). I knew that Sarah was a [designer](http://sarahbadr.com/), [artist](http://www.saatchi-gallery.co.uk/yourgallery/artist_profile/Sarah+Badr/22880.html), and a [writer](http://pieces-at-random.com/2007/12/26/commemoration/)... but I had no idea that she was also a musician, composing pieces under the moniker [**FRKTL**](http://frktl.com/). So you can imagine my surprise when I listened to her latest contribution on Sound Cloud, titled *Spöken,* and even bigger excitement when I totally fell in love with the track!!!

On this piece you can hear *"Cairene street field recording and overdubbed improv on a full-size electric violin with Whammy pedal".* Wow, this piece is sublime... And if you like this, there's pretty much a whole album available as a Sound Cloud set, [FRKTL](http://soundcloud.com/shbadr/sets/frktl). Totally blown away by this random finding... Hope you enjoy...
