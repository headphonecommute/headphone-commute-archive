### *The listener had no choice...*

### Release Notes

**Label:** Whales Records\
**Release:** *Insight IV*\
**Date:** February 15, 2019

[marchaljulien.fr](http://marchaljulien.fr/)

**Julien Marchal** avoids the names and titles and lets the music speak itself. For his full length, appropriately titled *INSIGHT IV* this French composer and pianist unveils ten fragile moments from deep within his soul. **Ludwig van Beethoven** questioned the meaning of music. He wondered what it was. He likened it to a sort of sorcery of higher power and hypnotism all at once. The music had the ability to bring the listener into the mental state of a composer. The listener had no choice. Playing music from Marchal I feel the very same way. And that is the true power of music.

In this exclusive track premiere, we feature "Insight XXXVII" for which Marchal [published the first page](https://www.facebook.com/julienmarchalmusic/photos/a.265351080310166/1130670120444920/) of the sheet music on social networks and asked his followers to improvise along. He has received eight different interpretations from the pianists all across the world. This, in a way, united certain characters together. And that is yet another power of music. I'm honoured to be a part of this family with these words.

The full album, *Insight IV*, is being released tomorrow, February 15, 2019, and is available directly from Marchal's [Bandcamp site](https://julienmarchal.bandcamp.com/album/insight-iv). Available on compact disc and 12" vinyl.
