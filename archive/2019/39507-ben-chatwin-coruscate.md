### Release Notes

**Label:** Village Green\
**Release:** Drone Signals\
**Date:** September 2018\
**See also:** [In the studio with Ben Chatwin](38104-in-the-studio-with-ben-chatwin.md)

[ben-chatwin.com](http://www.ben-chatwin.com/)

**Ben Chatwin**'s *Drone Signals* may have come out almost a year ago, but I am still listening to his music. Actually, I am also pretty excited at the opportunity to attend his live performance, here in London (more on that in a bit), and today, besides revisiting his catalogue, I'm happy to share with you this exclusive video for a piece from the album. The music video was produced by **Tatsuya Fujimoto** who's been credited for his visuals for **Constantine**, **Ryo Murakami**, and **Ryuichi Sakamoto**. This is a slow-panning visceral and mesmerising piece, which may accidentally evoke some emotions you've been successfully suppressing... until now. So here it is! Play it on full screen, of course, and don't forget to hear the entire album!

> I expressed gaze, light and darkness, sparkle through the microscope in this video.
>
> *- Tatsuya Fujimoto*

Now back to that live performance I mentioned above! It's actually part of a larger event, taking place on **Thursday, July 25th**, hosted by London's ***Southbank Centre*** and ***Village Green**.* Besides Ben Chatwin, the evening also features performances by **CUTS** and **Snow Palms** with the lighting design by **Icarus**. The event is titled ***IMMERSION*** and promises to be an evening of "expansive music and visuals" showcasing some of the label's output. I've got a flyer for you here. See you there!
