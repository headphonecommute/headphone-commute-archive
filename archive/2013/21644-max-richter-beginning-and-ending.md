About a decade ago, during an insomnia tortured night, I sat down in front of my laptop and clicked through the latest releases on Boomkat. This is when I first discovered **Max Richter**'s *The Blue Notebooks*. I specifically remember hearing myself saying out loud, *"What is this music?"* I didn't know, and couldn't possibly imagine, that since then, not only would I be able to meet Max in person, but that I would also be eternally grateful and honored for having him contributing a track towards **Headphone Commute**'s benefit compilation, *... and darkness came*. And now, here we are, premiering an exclusive track from Max Richter's upcoming release on **Milan Records** from his original soundtrack to *The Congress,* which comes out tomorrow, July 2nd, 2013. As you can see from the cover, this 2013 title by **Ari Folman** is a live action / animation film, which is a loose adaptation of **Stanislaw Lem's** 1971 sci-fi novel, *The Futurological Congress.* But today, you're here because of Max Richter's music. So here it is...
