**Ghostly** **International** has been on my rotations since the very first release. And almost religiously multiple releases from this Ann Arbor (Michigan) based label appear on Headphone Commute's **Best of The Year** special. The label was launched in 1999, and for over a decade has been at the forefront of innovative electronic sound, curating some of our all time favorite artists such as **Dabrye, Lusine, Solvent, Twine, Cepia,** and of course **Matthew Dear**. Around 2008 we watched as the label signed on even more interesting acts such as **The Sight Below, Mux Mool, Shigeto, Com Truise, Tycho, Aeroc** and **Jacaszek**. Today we officially erect our Ghostly International shrine!
