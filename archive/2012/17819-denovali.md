Welcome to the world of [**Denovali Records**](http://denovali.com), a Bochum (Germany) based independent label and mailorder shop, responsible for many of our favorite releases! The catalog of this imprint spans ambient, drone, darkjazz, post-rock and modern classical genres, somehow creating a unique umbrella for a perfect soundtrack. We've been following Denovali since its inception in 2006, and in the process watched it pick up many of our beloved artists, such as **Blueneck, Daturah, Her Name is Calla, Sankt Otten, Mouse On The Keys, Bersarin Quartett, The Samuel Jackson Five, The Mount Fuji Doomjazz Corporation, Blackfilm, Aun, Field Rotation, The Pirate Ship Quintet, thisquietarmy, Hidden Orchestra,** and many others! In the process, the label is also responsible for introducing us to such amazing acts like **SaffronKeira, Birds of Passage, Povarovo,** and **Celeste**.
